# Showtime revamp demo

Prototype of the revamped Showtime section (truevisions.co.th/magazine). Vanilla HTML/CSS/JS, no build step.

## IMPORTANT: large files – never read them whole
- `data/truevisions_content_categories_2026.csv` is 32 MB (3,787 articles). Inspect only with scripts (pandas, `head`), never open or attach it.
- `site/data/index.json` (~2 MB) and `site/data/articles/*.json` (~18 MB) are generated. Don't read them; query them with a short script if needed.

## Layout
- `site/index.html` – page shell (header, footer) and the template for every pre-rendered page; its `<!-- page-meta -->` block holds the site-wide title/share tags. Bump the `?v=` on styles.css/app.js after edits so deployed browsers fetch the new files
- `site/styles.css` – all styling; colour tokens at the top in `:root`
- `site/app.js` – path router (`/`, `/search?q=`, `/<section>`, `/<section>/<sub>`, `/shows/<hub>`, `/<section>/<article id>` like the old site; old `#a-…` links redirect) + page renderers: `home()`, `section()`, `hubPage()`, `article()`, `search()`
  - `SECTIONS` = the five sections and their tabs; `SHOWS` = show hubs; `WATCH_URL` = target of every "Watch on NOW" button
- `scripts/build_data.py` – CSV → `site/data/` (category mapping lives in `map_row()` and `SHOWS`)
- `scripts/build_titles.py` – TVS NOW catalog feeds → `site/data/titles.json` (article → real title for the article page's title card; stdlib only). Card links go to the peacock-clone app at `NOW_APP` in app.js (https://tvs-newhomepage.vercel.app; point it at http://localhost:5173 to test unpushed clone changes): the title card has one "ดูเลย" button (image and phone bar too) → `/watch/<type>/<id>`, the title's info page with its own play/subscribe buttons. The clone's `/play` gate is no longer used
- Article → NOW title, in priority order: `scripts/title_links.csv` (editor corrections; `none` = no title) → a NOW title link pasted in the article body (`…/watch/tvs-series/<id>`) → the title's name in the headline → `scripts/title_aliases.csv` (franchise keywords, e.g. โคนัน → the Conan series). No confident link → the page shows a generic "browse this section on NOW" card (`BROWSE` in app.js), never a guessed title. Each build writes `reports/title_review.csv` (last 60 days) for editors to check. Show hubs use their `now` id in `SHOWS`.
- `scripts/build_pages.mjs` – runs on every Vercel deploy (and in serve.js): writes the slim `site/data/list.json` the browser loads, `site/data/meta/<c>.json` (full summaries) and a pre-rendered `index.html` per section/hub/article with its share-preview tags. All git-ignored.
- `site/now-app/` – standalone mock of the TVS NOW mobile app home screen (phone frame on desktop, full screen on phones), drawn from `api/now-home.js`: the live `homepage_adult_th` feed (same one the clone's `/api/tvs-feed` proxies), trimmed. Continue Watching / My List are per-user and empty in the feed, so the mock borrows titles from other shelves. เดิม/ใหม่/ใหม่ 2 switch (`LOOK`, `?look=classic|new|apple`; ใหม่ 2 = ใหม่ with an Apple TV-style full-bleed hero, `.hc-apple`). The ใหม่ hero comes from `api/now-hero.js` (TMDB trending: textless posters + logos, linking to the clone's `/watch/movie|tv/<id>`); it needs a `TMDB_API_KEY` env var (Vercel project settings; locally export it before `node scripts/serve.js`, e.g. from peacock-clone's `.env.local`). Without a key it falls back to the feed's hero
- `site/now-appCP/` – True/CP privilege concept on top of now-app (reuses its app.js/app.css, pinned to ใหม่ 2 via `<body data-look>`; listens for app.js's `now-home` event and adds shelf kinds via `EXTRA_SHELVES`). `cp.js`/`cp.css` move ใหม่บน NOW into the header pills, turn that tab into True Privilege, a members' club (welcome back + member since, CI membership card with TruePoint and level, สิทธิ์ของฉัน (`ENTITLEMENTS`, folded to one summary line – `.pv-fold` details; the CP partner list further down uses the same fold but starts open: each right, its source plan, the apps it plays on, active/activating), the True Exclusive shelf, members-only `EVENTS` with seats to book, ready-to-use `REWARDS` tickets, unlocked rails, CP partner benefits, then a สะสมแต้มเพิ่ม group with TruePoint points/level, daily check-in, watch-to-earn meter (`WATCH` milestones, True x2), quests (the first is `MISSION`: finish The Voice EP.4 before the live show for a free All Café coffee; the True Exclusive rail shows it as a strip that resumes the episode on tap (`missionStrip`, `playMission`; `player()` takes `start`/`progress`/`end`/`close`), and finishing it adds the coffee to the rewards); in-memory demo state) and add a True Exclusive rail under the hero. A ดูในฐานะ switch (`?as=true|cp|sim|ais`, `PERSONAS`/`RANK` in cp.js; each privilege needs level 1 = CP member or Voice SIM, 2 = True postpaid; `sim` = The Voice SIM, a prepaid content SIM sold at 7-Eleven, with its own entitlements; `simFlow` sells it in the app as an eSIM through the True app (pay + e-KYC there) or a 7-Eleven pickup barcode, offered only for The Voice titles and on the card) and a ไลน์อัพ switch (`?lineup=th|intl`: Thai-first AF/originals per the CEO's direction – The Voice is kept off the rail for its own home section, and the Thai lineup's 7-Eleven poster is The Voice, or the international HBO slate) shows non-members locked posters, a live "True customers watching" count and a join sheet (switch to True / ALL member free) that flips the persona. Privilege wears the True | dtac CI (red → magenta → violet → blue gradient + light ribbon, `--ci` in cp.css), not NOW magenta. The rail's titles are HBO series from `api/now-hbo.js` (TMDB, same key as now-hero), or the feed shelves in `PREMIUM_SHELVES` without TMDB; on screen they're only called ซีรีส์พรีเมียม (no HBO naming). The 7-Eleven loop: an in-store poster in the demo panel (`drawStore`, desktop only) → `scanSheet` (camera finds the QR) → `claimSheet` → `player` (mock, 1 s = 2 min; members' minutes feed `addWatch` and toast TruePoint; non-members get episode 1 free, then pay with ALL member points / TrueMoney / a 7-Eleven counter barcode, or switch to True). cp.js shares globals with app.js, so check new top-level names don't clash. CP partner logos live in `site/img/cp/` (sources in the `CP` comment in cp.js). The Voice hub (`voiceHub()`, `VOICE_*`): the franchise in one overlay: the season (timeline = round picker, episodes per round), coaches' teams and singers to follow (`COACHES`/`SINGERS`, labelled examples – the feed has no such data), performance shorts with a share sheet (`voiceShorts`, free to all), predictions that win a front-row concert seat + meeting the coaches (`PRED`, added to the tour tickets), a live-vote mock (`liveVote`), backstage, past seasons, and the CP-store tour with tickets (`tourSheet`: free seat for SIM/True, else ALL member points / TrueMoney / 7-Eleven counter); hub state in `VH`, opened from the home's The Voice brand section (`EXTRA_SHELVES.voice_brand`, app.js's Lemino-style `.hub-solo` markup in The Voice red, under True Exclusive), The Voice posters on the rail and the SIM's entitlements; `voiceAll()` = True or SIM. Test variants for user tests (demo panel, desktop): `?poster=full|blur` (locked posters) and `?offer=privilege|price` (a plain ฿149 package; buying sets `PAID`, which `can()` counts for content while `member()` keeps the club's rewards/events/points locked); `?clean` hides the demo panel for sessions; the store poster's พิมพ์สำหรับทดสอบ link prints it at A5 as a session prop (`printPoster`)
- `api/live.js` – Vercel function: upcoming/live broadcasts from the NOW sport feed for the Sports pages' fixtures list
- `vercel.json` – build command, SPA rewrite, and Vercel image resizing for truevisions.co.th covers (`sized()` in app.js, Vercel hosts only)
- `site/artifact.html` – old claude.ai demo copy; still hash-based and out of date

## Run
`node scripts/serve.js` → http://localhost:8000 (builds the generated files, serves real paths and `api/`)

## Rebuild data after changing the mapping
`python3 scripts/build_data.py data/truevisions_content_categories_2026.csv site/data`
then `python3 scripts/build_titles.py site/data` (also re-run it to refresh titles from the live catalog)

## Design rules
- Two looks behind the เดิม/ใหม่ switch in the demo ribbon (`LOOK` in app.js; `?look=tudum|classic`, remembered per browser; default classic). The Tudum-style look is everything scoped under `body.tudum` in styles.css plus `homeTudum()`; `homeClassic()` is the current home.
- Header: white sticky masthead modelled on the NOW Blog header in peacock-clone (`src/components/NowBlogHeader.jsx`) – logo row folds away on scroll on desktop, phones get one bar plus a tab drawer. The old magenta truevisions.co.th top bar was dropped.
- Page body keeps the current truevisions.co.th Showtime look: pink headings (#F2408E), white cards with pink/purple gradient borders, font Better Together (the TrueVisions brand font, self-hosted in site/fonts/ from now.truevisions.co.th; covers Thai).
- Brand CTA colours from the TVS NOW design system: magenta #FB008D, brand gradient #FB008D → #FF0000.
- Copy is Thai-first; keep English labels where the live site uses them (Highlight, Sports, See More).
